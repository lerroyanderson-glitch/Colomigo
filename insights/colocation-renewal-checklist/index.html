BUYER GUIDE / COLO-MIGO STAFF

Colocation Renewal Checklist

Build a renewal decision around verified dates and requirements. Use this checklist to gather the facts before discussing a new term with your provider or comparing alternatives.

IN THIS GUIDE

  1. Start with the notice deadline
  2. Measure the environment you are renewing
  3. Compare the full next term
  4. Make the decision reviewable

What to leave with

A dated requirements brief, a consistent cost comparison, and a list of questions with named owners.

01 / CHECKLIST

Start with the notice deadline

Locate the current agreement, amendments, service orders, and recent invoices. Ask your authorized contract team to confirm the expiry date, notice deadline, required delivery method, recipient, and any automatic renewal provisions. Put the verified dates on the decision calendar.

The notice deadline and service expiry are separate milestones. Work backward from the earlier decision deadline to allow for internal approvals, provider responses, and any alternative deployment. There is no single notice period that applies to every agreement.

  • Record the document and section supporting each date.
  • Name the person who may authorize and send a notice.
  • Keep proof of any authorized notice and the provider’s acknowledgment.

02 / CHECKLIST

Measure the environment you are renewing

Create one inventory of cabinets or cage space, installed equipment, committed power, measured load, circuits, cross-connects, and support services. Keep current usage separate from the capacity you pay for. Have the technical owner confirm units, feed design, and usable capacity.

  • What must stay unchanged for the next term?
  • Which workloads may grow, shrink, or move?
  • What evidence supports the forecast, and who owns it?
  • Which access, support, security, or reporting requirements must a proposal address?

Provider products may be ordered separately. For example, Equinix lists space, physical interconnection, and on-site support as distinct offerings. Check the actual order and invoice rather than assuming the cabinet rate covers every service. See Equinix’s product documentation.

03 / CHECKLIST

Compare the full next term

Ask for the renewal proposal in writing. Use the same capacity, service assumptions, currency, and evaluation period for each available option. Separate recurring charges from installation, migration, and other one-time charges.

  • Check space, power billing, connectivity, cross-connects, and support charges.
  • List escalators, variable usage, taxes or pass-through charges, and exclusions.
  • Record contract term, billing start, quote expiry, and delivery dependencies.
  • If considering a move, add overlap between old and new services and separately scoped migration costs.

Mark unquoted items as unknown. A missing price is not a zero cost. Use the colocation quote comparison guide to structure the review.

04 / CHECKLIST

Make the decision reviewable

Bring the current-provider proposal and any available alternatives into one decision memo. Compare operational fit, total evaluated cost, timing, unresolved risks, and what would change for the team. Renewing in place can be a suitable outcome.

Example: a lower monthly offer is not yet a comparable alternative if its usable power, cross-connect charges, and ready-for-service date remain unconfirmed. Assign those questions before recommending a choice.

  • Identify the preferred option and the reasons it meets requirements.
  • Assign an owner and due date to every unresolved item.
  • Have the authorized team review the final agreement and any notice.
  • Record approval, provider acceptance, and the next implementation milestone separately.

Turn the checklist into a next step.

Bring your requirements and open questions to a conversation with the Colo-Migo team. Start with a high-level outline; agree how to share confidential documents before sending them.
