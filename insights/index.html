01 POWER

Power is no longer just a line item.

Capacity requests, grid flexibility, and energy constraints are shaping deployment plans. When comparing facilities, ask what is energized now, reserved for your project, or still planned.

Ask next: What is the earliest contractually supported service date for your required kW?

02 MARKET

Revisit the economics before renewing.

Digital Realty reported higher cash renewal rental rates in its third quarter. Review total recurring cost, escalators, notice dates, and credible alternatives—not just the quoted base rate.

Ask next: Which charges change with the new term?

Source: Digital Realty, Oct 29, 2026 ↗
03 AI-READY

Higher-density racks change the conversation.

Uptime Institute's 2026 survey reports more facilities with peak rack densities of 30 kW or higher. Confirm the real cooling method, rack-level distribution, and operational support.

Ask next: Can the proposed hall support your actual load?
