001 / THE EDITORIAL DESK

Availability is a delivery commitment.

By: Colo-Migo Staff
Issue 001 · November 3, 2026

A capacity announcement can start a conversation. A buyer still needs to know which facility, how much usable power, and when the service can be delivered. Colo-Migo’s analysis starts with those delivery questions.

The distinction matters when comparing proposals. A project in development, a completed facility, and power ready for a specific deployment represent different stages of readiness. Each calls for a different set of questions.

  • Power: What capacity is energized and available for your load?
  • Timing: Which milestones support the proposed service date?
  • Terms: What does the agreement commit the provider to deliver?

A useful capacity comparison also accounts for the buyer’s move, carrier turn-up, testing, and renewal notice dates. Making those dependencies visible gives the team a schedule it can evaluate before making a commitment.
