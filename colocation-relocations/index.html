01 / Start with the essentials

What to bring to the conversation.

Origin and destination

Current facility, preferred destination market, cabinets or cage space, equipment footprint, power density, and redundancy needs.

Network and readiness

Carriers, bandwidth, cloud connections, cross-connects, latency or diversity requirements, and provider readiness questions.

Timing and ownership

Target-live date, current contract notice dates, move window, budget, and owners for equipment transport, migration, testing, and rollback.

02 / A defined engagement

What you’ll receive.

Deliverables and coordination scope are agreed for your project.

Relocation requirements brief

A written summary of destination needs, current-environment constraints, priorities, timing, and unresolved technical or commercial questions.

Facility and connectivity comparison

Available provider proposals compared by recurring and one-time costs, power, circuits and cross-connects, contract term, delivery timing, and exclusions.

Move coordination plan

An agreed plan for moving-provider sourcing, facility and network readiness, responsibilities, milestones and handoffs.

Moving-provider sourcing and relocation coordination can be included in your agreed project scope. Physical transport is performed by the selected mover. Network design, migration, testing and rollback are assigned to your team or appointed specialists. Scope, fees and provider delivery dates are confirmed before work begins.

03 / Practical questions

Before your next step.

Can you find movers and coordinate our relocation?

Yes. We can source specialist data center movers, gather proposals and coordinate the agreed move with your team and providers. The selected moving company handles physical transport; your technical team or appointed specialists handle migration, testing and rollback. We agree scope, responsibilities and fees with you before work begins.

How do you compare destination facilities?

We use your requirements to compare available proposals, including space, power, connectivity, costs, contract terms, delivery assumptions, and exclusions. Provider claims and availability need confirmation for your specific project.

Can you help with a relocation anywhere in the U.S.?

Yes. We help source colocation, connectivity and specialist moving options nationwide. Share your origin, destination, equipment needs and target date so we can explore available providers and plan the next steps.

What should we send before the first call?

A high-level summary is enough: origin and destination, approximate cabinets and power, network needs, and target-live date. Avoid sending credentials or sensitive infrastructure diagrams in an initial inquiry. Project scope, fees, and applicable provider compensation should be clarified before engagement.
