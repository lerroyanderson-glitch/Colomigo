02 / The advisory process

Know what you’ll receive.

  1. 01

    Your requirements brief

    A written summary of your location, space, power, network, budget, and timing. A structured assessment can bring technical questions forward when needed.

    An agreed basis for sourcing
  2. 02

    Your quote comparison

    Research suitable providers, request project-specific proposals, and compare one-time and recurring costs, term, timing, and material exclusions. Confirm any preliminary pricing before a decision.

    Clear tradeoffs and assumptions
  3. 03

    Your next-step plan

    A practical list of decisions, owners, and milestones. The selected provider contracts with, bills, and delivers the service; Colo-Migo helps keep agreed follow-through visible.

    Defined responsibilities and follow-through

Available services and fit are confirmed for your project.

Colo-Migo uses provider research tools and specialist sales engineering support to explore options when a project needs them. The Colo-Migo team remains the point of contact as options are sourced, clarified, and compared.

  • More options to explore. Research facilities and connectivity across providers, then confirm availability and fit for your project.
  • Technical questions addressed. Use structured discovery and specialist input when a solution needs deeper review.
  • A clearer comparison. Separate early budget estimates from firm provider quotes and review the costs, terms, and tradeoffs.
  • A defined handoff. Know which decisions belong to you, Colo-Migo, and the selected provider as the service moves toward delivery.

Provider availability, capacity, terms, and pricing are confirmed for each project. The selected provider contracts with and bills the customer. Colo-Migo may receive compensation when a provider agreement is placed.
