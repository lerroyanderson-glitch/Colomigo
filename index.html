01 / What we do

How can we help?

Renewing in place or moving to a new facility? Start with your space, power, network, budget, and timing. We turn those requirements into a practical provider comparison.

Colocation renewals

Review your current requirements and compare available options before your renewal decision.

  • Expiry and notice dates to confirm
  • Current spend, capacity, and growth needs
  • Renewal proposal and alternative quotes

Colocation relocations

Source facilities that fit your next location, technical needs, and target-live date.

  • Cabinets, cages, power, and redundancy
  • Market, connectivity, and compliance needs
  • Provider availability and delivery timing

Connectivity & coordination

Compare network options and keep the agreed provider milestones and responsibilities visible.

  • Carrier, cloud, and cross-connect options
  • Provider and stakeholder follow-through
  • Coordination within an agreed scope

02 / How we work

Know what you’ll receive.

A requirements brief, a side-by-side comparison of available quotes, and a clear action plan. Deliverables and coordination scope are agreed for your project.

  1. 01

    Your requirements brief

    A written summary of your location, space, power, network, budget, and timing, with open questions and dates to confirm.

    An agreed basis for sourcing
  2. 02

    Your quote comparison

    Available provider proposals compared by one-time and recurring costs, term, escalators, timing, and material exclusions.

    Clear tradeoffs and assumptions
  3. 03

    Your next-step plan

    A practical list of decisions, owners, and milestones. For relocations, clarify who owns the move, testing, and handoff.

    Defined responsibilities and follow-through

Your privacy.

Colo-Migo does not use analytics or advertising cookies on this page and does not store project-brief entries. The brief tool prepares an email through your own email application; you choose whether to send it.

If you email Colo-Migo, we use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project. Please avoid sending passwords, confidential provider documents, or sensitive infrastructure details in an initial inquiry.

Google Fonts and an Unsplash-hosted image are loaded from external providers, which receive normal connection information. Your email provider and the website hosting provider may also process technical connection information under their respective policies.
