Your privacy.

Colo-Migo does not use analytics or advertising cookies on this page and does not store project-brief entries. The brief tool prepares an email through your own email application; you choose whether to send it.

If you email Colo-Migo, we use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project. Please avoid sending passwords, confidential provider documents, or sensitive infrastructure details in an initial inquiry.

Google Fonts and an Unsplash-hosted image are loaded from external providers, which receive normal connection information. Your email provider and the website hosting provider may also process technical connection information under their respective policies.

If you choose online booking, Calendly processes the information you enter and may use cookies under its own privacy policy. The booking calendar is loaded only when you choose to open it. You can also arrange a call by email.
