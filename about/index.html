A LESSON FROM DATA CENTER OPERATIONS

Understand the service before changing it.

One cross-connect review involved records that appeared to show a disconnect alongside an active service. I worked through the history with colleagues to distinguish billing changes from the service itself. The customer’s next decision also depended on understanding their own network dependencies.

That experience reinforced how I approach a complex request: check the records, involve the right people, and make clear what has been confirmed and what still needs an answer. Those are the habits I bring to a business relationship.

02 / WHY COLO-MIGO EXISTS

A clearer path from need to decision.

Our mission is to help businesses make informed colocation and connectivity decisions through clear comparisons, practical guidance, and dependable follow-through.

Colo-Migo brings together the parts of my career I care about most: understanding a customer’s needs, explaining the tradeoffs, and keeping the next step moving. I built it to give buyers a consistent person to work with as they evaluate a renewal, relocation, or new capacity requirement. Through AVANT’s Pathfinder tools and sales engineering resources, I can research a wider set of provider options and bring specialists into a project when needed. The selected provider owns the service contract, billing, and delivery.
