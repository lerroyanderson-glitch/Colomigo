A LESSON FROM DATA CENTER OPERATIONS

Understand the service before changing it.

In a previous data center role, LeRoy reviewed records that appeared to show a disconnect alongside an active cross-connect service. He worked through the history with colleagues to distinguish billing changes from the service itself. The customer’s next decision also depended on understanding their own network dependencies.

The review reinforced the importance of checking records, involving the right people, and separating confirmed facts from open questions. LeRoy brings those habits to his work at Colo-Migo, where clear requirements and defined responsibilities guide the advisory process.

02 / WHY COLO-MIGO EXISTS

A clearer path from need to decision.

Colo-Migo’s mission is to help businesses make informed colocation and connectivity decisions through clear comparisons, practical guidance, and dependable follow-through.

Colo-Migo was established to give buyers a consistent advisory point of contact as they evaluate a renewal, relocation, or new capacity requirement. The team focuses on understanding requirements, explaining tradeoffs, and organizing the next steps. Provider research tools and sales engineering resources support a wider review of options, with specialist input when a project needs it. The selected provider owns the service contract, billing, and delivery.
