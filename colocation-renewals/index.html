Is your current colocation still the right fit?

A renewal is a chance to review more than the monthly price. Your power needs, network connections, growth plans, and support requirements may have changed. A useful comparison starts with those requirements and the dates in your existing agreement.

We help build a consistent basis for discussing your renewal and requesting available alternatives. Staying with your current provider may be a suitable outcome; the goal is a decision you can explain against your priorities.

01 / Start with the essentials

What to bring to the conversation.

Dates and contract contact

Current expiry date, notice deadline, auto-renewal language, and the person authorized to communicate contract decisions.

Current service and spend

Cabinets or cage space, committed and used power, circuits, cross-connects, recurring charges, and any upcoming changes.

Your next term

Expected growth, preferred contract length, budget, location needs, and any operational or compliance requirements to confirm.

02 / A defined engagement

What you’ll receive.

Deliverables and coordination scope are agreed for your project.

Requirements brief

A written summary of your current environment, future needs, priorities, open questions, and renewal dates to verify.

Proposal comparison

Available renewal and alternative proposals compared by recurring and one-time costs, power and connectivity charges, term, escalators, timing, and exclusions.

Decision and next-step plan

A list of outstanding provider questions, decisions, owners, and milestones so your team can move the selected option forward.

Your team confirms contractual dates and authorizes any notice or agreement. Colo-Migo does not send cancellation notices or commit you to a provider without authorization. Any deployment coordination is defined separately in the project scope.

03 / Practical questions

Before your next step.

When should we start a renewal review?

Start with the notice deadline in your current agreement, not only its expiry date. Confirm that deadline and your internal approval process first, then allow time to gather requirements, obtain proposals, and evaluate any relocation dependencies. The appropriate schedule depends on your contract and project.

Can we stay with our current provider?

Yes. A review can support renewing in place or evaluating alternatives. We compare the available proposals against your requirements; a relocation is not assumed.

Do you guarantee savings or cover every provider?

No savings or exhaustive market coverage is promised. Options depend on provider access, availability, and the project requirements. The comparison should make its coverage, assumptions, and exclusions clear.

What happens in the first conversation?

We discuss your market, current capacity, renewal timing, and priorities. You can begin with approximate information. Project deliverables, fees, and applicable provider compensation should be clarified before you authorize an engagement.
